_Q7Leader is a cloud-based HR intelligence platform that enables managers to create objective employee profiles in under 10 minutes by answering seven structured, peer-validated questions—reducing bias and streamlining performance evaluations . These profiles feed into intuitive dashboards offering "People P&L" analytics, helping leaders track talent strengths, skill gaps, engagement, succession readiness, and fair compensation decisions. Designed to modernize HR processes, the platform supports collaborative talent reviews, succession planning, transparent reward mechanisms, and compliance—all from one centralized interface._

This article provides an overview to get started with the **_Q7Leader_** connector in **Peliqan**. Please [contact support](https://peliqan.io/contact) if you have any additional questions or remarks.

## Get API Token

- **Log in** to the account at `[my.q7leader.com](http://my.q7leader.com)` using manager/admin credentials.
- Navigate to the **Integration** or **API Tokens** section, usually accessible from your profile dropdown or the Admin/Settings menu.
- Click **"Generate Token"** .
- Provide a unique **label/name** (e.g. "Peliqan Token").
- Select the appropriate **permissions** or **scopes,** ensure at least read access for endpoints like `/member/{member}`.
- Click **Save** or **Create**.
