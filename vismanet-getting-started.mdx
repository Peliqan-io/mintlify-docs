This article provides an overview to get started with the **Visma net** connector in **Peliqan**. Please [contact support](https://peliqan.io/contact) if you have any additional questions or remarks.

## Connect Visma.net

Follow the below steps.

### 1. Enable the Visma app store

Make sure the Visma App Store is activated for your Company:

![Visma Net App Store activation toggle](https://images.spr.so/cdn-cgi/imagedelivery/j42No7y-dcokJuNgXeA0ig/139861be-5ff3-4228-89b5-477dddf48546/visma_net_activate_app_store/w=1920,quality=90,fit=scale-down)

### 2. Add role "Integration Administrator" to your user

Make sure you have the role "Integration Administrator" assigned to your user:

![Visma Net Integration Administrator user role assignment](https://images.spr.so/cdn-cgi/imagedelivery/j42No7y-dcokJuNgXeA0ig/5265891a-f0f8-4004-adde-459036fddc55/visma_net_integration_administrator_user_role/w=1920,quality=90,fit=scale-down)

### 3. Add the Peliqan app to your tenant

Add the Peliqan app to your Visma net tenant. Go to the Visma app store:

![Visma Net App Store page](https://images.spr.so/cdn-cgi/imagedelivery/j42No7y-dcokJuNgXeA0ig/e36d2b94-4423-4683-adb9-16d83f2285f0/visma_net_app_store/w=1920,quality=90,fit=scale-down)

Go to "My apps" and click on the button "Use invitation code":

![Visma Net App Store invitation code button](https://images.spr.so/cdn-cgi/imagedelivery/j42No7y-dcokJuNgXeA0ig/ec1aeef2-9d1a-40f4-8d47-426d25cee757/visma_net_app_store_invitation_code/w=1920,quality=90,fit=scale-down)

Enter the invitation code that you received from Peliqan support.

In the next steps, approve the different scopes that are shown.
