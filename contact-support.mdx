Our customer support team is standing by to help you!

Send an email with your request. This will create a support ticket, and we will get back to you based on your license tier:

[**support@peliqan.io**](mailto:support@peliqan.io)

If you have a dedicated account manager, you can also contact them directly by email or phone.

You can manage your open support tickets in the Peliqan helpdesk portal: [https://helpdesk.peliqan.io/](https://helpdesk.peliqan.io/)

<Warning>
  Please make sure to include all information related to your problem, including screenshots or a copy/paste of error messages or log file entries. Peliqan support does not have access to your account by default.
</Warning>

We look forward to hearing from you!
