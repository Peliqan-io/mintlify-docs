This article provides an overview to get started with the **Linkedin Ads** connector in **Peliqan**. Please [contact support](https://peliqan.io/contact) if you have any additional questions or remarks.

## Connect

In Peliqan, go to Connections, click on "Add Connection" and select Linkedin Ads.

### Organization id

Enter your Linkedin Organization id. Note: this is NOT your Linkedin Ads Account id.

Go to your Linkedin company page, make sure you are logged in as Admin of your company. You will find your organization id in the URL. In the example below, the organization id is **84313123**.

  src="/images/Linkedin Ads - Getting started in Peliqan/Linkedin_organization_id.png"

/>

### Account id

Enter the id of your Linkedin Ads account, you can it in the top left corner of the Linkedin Campaign Manager: [https://www.linkedin.com/campaignmanager/](https://www.linkedin.com/campaignmanager/). In the example below, the Account id is **510767456**.
