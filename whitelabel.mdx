# Choosing between Whitelabel and Embedding

Peliqan.io support different service models for ISVs, SaaS companies and consultants (Partners). Depending on your service model, you can choose between Whitelabel and Embedding.

## Full-service

The Professional Services team or Customer Success team of the Partner takes care of setting up data solutions for end-customers.

They can use the Peliqan platform to setup end-customer accounts, activate solutions and/or build out custom solutions.

→ typically no need for Embedding or the Partner API. Optional [White-labeling](/whitelabel/whitelabel) of the Peliqan platform to enabled self-service access by end-customers.

## Self-service

End-customers of the Partner need to activate data solutions in a self-service manner .

→ use Peliqan [Embedding](/whitelabel/embedding) and the [Partner API](/whitelabel/partner-api-embed-api).
