This article provides an overview to get started with the **Microsoft Planner** connector in **Peliqan**. Please [contact support](https://peliqan.io/contact) if you have any additional questions or remarks.

## Connect Microsoft Planner in Peliqan

In Peliqan, go to Connections > Add New, and search for "Microsoft Planner" in the list.

Click on the button "Connect Microsoft Planner".

<img
  src="/images/Microsoft Planner - Getting started in Peliqan/Screenshot_2025-10-20_at_10.50.12.png"
  alt="Microsoft Planner connection in Peliqan"
/>

In the next page, log in with your Microsoft account and authorize access for Peliqan to your Microsoft Planner data.
