The Peliqan extension for Business Central allows to set up efficient data pipelines that sync data from your Business Central tenant to your data warehouse.

The extension is a read-only extension that exposes secure API endpoints. These API endpoints are used by the data pipelines to read data from Business Central, including standard & custom objects, as well as standard & custom fields.

## How to install the extension

Follow the below steps to install the extension in one environment in your Business Central tenant:

Open search (right top corner, magnifier icon) and search for **Extension Management**. Click on the link **Extension Management**:

<img
  src="/images/MS Dynamics 365 Business Central - Getting started/MS Business Central extension/Screenshot_2026-05-18_at_10.17.47.png"
  alt="image"
/>

Click on **Manage** &gt; **Upload Extension**:

<img
  src="/images/MS Dynamics 365 Business Central - Getting started/MS Business Central extension/Screenshot_2026-05-18_at_10.17.59.png"
  alt="image"
/>

Download this zip file and unzip it to get the `.app` file:

[bc-peliqan-sync-v1.0.0.8.zip](https://github.com/Peliqan-io/downloads/releases/download/d/bc-peliqan-sync-v1.0.0.8.zip) (12.9 KiB)

Upload the `.app` file in the field "Select .app file".

Make sure to toggle the approval toggle "By deploying this extension you acknowledge that your data may be sent outside...".

Click on **Deploy**:

<img
  src="/images/MS Dynamics 365 Business Central - Getting started/MS Business Central extension/Screenshot_2026-05-18_at_10.18.28.png"
  alt="image"
  className="mx-auto"
  style={{ width:"66%" }}
/>

## After installation: get all details

Once the extension has been installed, please note following settings, so that the data pipeline can be configured (see below):

- Tenant ID
- Environment name
- Company ID

You can find these details in the extension, by following the below steps:

Open search (right top corner, magnifier icon) and search for "Peliqan". Click on the link "Peliqan Sync - API endpoint" from the search results:

<img
  src="/images/MS Dynamics 365 Business Central - Getting started/MS Business Central extension/Screenshot_2026-05-18_at_10.19.20.png"
  alt="image"
/>

This will open the UI of the extension:

<img
  src="/images/MS Dynamics 365 Business Central - Getting started/MS Business Central extension/Screenshot_2026-05-18_at_10.20.07.png"
  alt="image"
/>

Scroll down to **Connection details** and copy the required settings:

<img
  src="/images/MS Dynamics 365 Business Central - Getting started/MS Business Central extension/Screenshot_2026-05-18_at_10.20.14.png"
  alt="image"

## Add a connection in Peliqan

In Peliqan, add a connection of type "**MS Business Central (Peliqan extension)**". Enter the above details in the connection card:

<img
  src="/images/MS Dynamics 365 Business Central - Getting started/MS Business Central extension/Screenshot_2026-09-10_at_11.20.55.png"
  alt="image"
/>

Click on the "Add connection" button and authorize access (OAuth flow).

Once the connection is added, all data will be synced to the data warehouse.

If you want to add more tables, check the table `Objects` for correct object names:

<img
  src="/images/MS Dynamics 365 Business Central - Getting started/MS Business Central extension/objects_tabel.png"
  alt="image"
/>

Use the object names from column `objectname` and add them in the connection card under "Object names" (comma separated list). Example:

<img
  src="/images/MS Dynamics 365 Business Central - Getting started/MS Business Central extension/connection_card.png"
  alt="image"
/>

After updating this list, perform a Full Resync to make all the tables (including the newly selected objects) available.

<img
  src="/images/MS Dynamics 365 Business Central - Getting started/MS Business Central extension/full_resync.png"
  alt="image"
  className="mx-auto"
  style={{ width:"46%" }}
/>
