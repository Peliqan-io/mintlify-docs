title: "Salesforce CRM Analytics"
---

Salesforce CRM Analytics is a BI tool that allows you to work with data from Peliqan:

<img
  src="/images/Connect your BI, DB tool or iPaaS/Salesforce CRM Analytics/image.png"
  alt="image.png"
/>

Make sure to use a **Bigquery** data warehouse in Peliqan, if you want to use Salesforce CRM Analytics as a BI tool.

Activate the Bigquery data warehouse under Connections. Click on “Add data warehouse”:

<img
  src="/images/Connect your BI, DB tool or iPaaS/Salesforce CRM Analytics/Screenshot_2026-07-03_at_15.47.49.png"
  alt="Screenshot 2026-07-03 at 15.47.49.png"
  style={{ width:"41%" }}
/>

Click on “Provision now”, in the card “Peliqan Bigquery data warehouse”:

<img
  src="/images/Connect your BI, DB tool or iPaaS/Salesforce CRM Analytics/Screenshot_2026-07-03_at_15.49.45.png"
  alt="Screenshot 2026-07-03 at 15.49.45.png"
/>

Once the Bigquery data warehouse is ready, make sure to add new source Connections and select Bigquery as the target, so that your data resides in Bigquery.

Next, go to your Bigquery connection card:

<img
  src="/images/Connect your BI, DB tool or iPaaS/Salesforce CRM Analytics/Screenshot_2026-07-03_at_15.48.03.png"
  alt="Screenshot 2026-07-03 at 15.48.03.png"
  style={{ width:"47%" }}
/>

Copy the password:

<img
  src="/images/Connect your BI, DB tool or iPaaS/Salesforce CRM Analytics/Screenshot_2026-07-03_at_15.48.10.png"
  alt="Screenshot 2026-07-03 at 15.48.10.png"
  style={{ width:"41%" }}
/>
